About the Event

The DMV's Premier Showcase

The DMV All Met Cup is the region's definitive all-star match — a prestigious annual showcase pitting the top public school talent against the DMV's elite private school programs. This isn't a tournament. It's a statement.


Designed with college coaches in mind, every player on the field represents the best of DC, Maryland, and Virginia — selected through a competitive evaluation process across the DMV.
